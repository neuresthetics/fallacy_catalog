## Conclusion first

Corporations run the **same lever stack** as public BI (Defaults, Salience, Norms, Affect, Messenger, Incentives, Commitments, Priming*) — almost never under the word **MINDSPACE**. They brand it *behavioural science / choice architecture / digital nudge / CRO / OCA*. Regulators now litigate the **mechanics** as dark patterns / deceptive design / sludge, not “clever UX.”

\*Priming = low-confidence in commercial use (replication).

## Effect-size caveat

**DellaVigna & Linos:** academic nudge RCTs average ~**8pp**; at-scale / gov multi-trial averages closer to **~1.4pp**. Treat vendor “+180% / +64%” claims as marketing-grade unless you see design + sample + pre-reg.
